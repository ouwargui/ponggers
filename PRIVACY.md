# Privacy Policy for Ponggers

**Effective date:** July 17, 2026

Ponggers is designed to be played without creating an account or entering your
name or contact details. Ponggers does not contain advertising, use tracking
technologies, build user profiles, or use analytics to study player behavior.

## Information handled by Ponggers

### Game preferences

Settings such as game preferences are stored locally on your device. They are
not transmitted to Ponggers servers. You can remove them by resetting the
app's settings or deleting the app.

### Online matches

If you choose to play an online match, Ponggers must process limited technical
information to connect you with another player. This may include:

- a temporary, randomly generated connection identifier;
- the temporary room code you create or join;
- your IP address and basic network or connection metadata; and
- WebRTC connection-negotiation messages.

This information is used only to create the room, establish the peer-to-peer
connection, prevent abuse, and keep the online service working. Ponggers does
not use it for advertising, tracking, profiling, or analytics.

Gameplay data is normally exchanged directly between the two players through
an encrypted WebRTC connection. When a direct connection is not possible,
Cloudflare TURN may relay the encrypted network traffic between players.

Ponggers does not request your name, email address, phone number, contacts,
photos, precise location, or payment information. The game does not use your
camera or microphone.

## Retention

Room and signaling information exists only as needed for the online session.
It is removed when players disconnect or the room expires. A room waiting for
an opponent expires after five minutes, and an active match room expires within
two hours.

The online service is hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process network
metadata, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect its services.
Technical diagnostic logs may be retained for up to seven days. For more
information, see [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Ponggers does not maintain player accounts, persistent online profiles, match
histories, or cloud game saves.

## Sharing and sale of information

Ponggers does not sell or rent personal information. Limited technical data is
processed by Cloudflare only as necessary to provide, secure, and diagnose the
online multiplayer service. Ponggers does not share data with advertisers or
data brokers.

## Children's privacy

Ponggers does not knowingly solicit names, contact details, or similar personal
information from children, and it does not offer player accounts or social
profiles.

## Your choices

You can play solo or local multiplayer without connecting to the Ponggers
online service. You can remove locally stored preferences by resetting the
app's settings or deleting the app. Because Ponggers has no accounts or
persistent player database, there is normally no user profile or stored match
history to access or delete.

## Changes to this policy

If Ponggers' data practices change, this policy will be updated before those
changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page will also be
updated.
